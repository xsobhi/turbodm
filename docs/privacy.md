# TurboDM privacy policy

TurboDM is a download manager that runs on your computer. Its browser extension, *TurboDM
Integration*, hands the downloads you start in your browser to the TurboDM app on the same computer.

**What the extension reads.** When you download a file, the extension reads that download's
address, its file name, the page it came from, your browser's user-agent and that site's cookies.

**Where that goes.** Only to the TurboDM app on your own computer, through the browser's native
messaging. It is not sent to us or anyone else. The app sends the cookies only to the server the
file is downloaded from, as your browser would, so downloads that need you to be signed in work.

**What is stored.** The extension stores its own settings in your browser: catching on or off, a
minimum file size and the sites to leave alone. The app keeps your download list and settings on
your computer.

**What we collect.** Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. Besides your
downloads, the app only asks GitHub for the latest release (at start and once a day) to tell you
about updates. Turn *Check for updates* off in the app's options to stop that.

**Questions.** Open an issue at <https://github.com/xsobhi/turbodm/issues>.
